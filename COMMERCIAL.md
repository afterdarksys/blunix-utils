# Commercial license

Blunix's own code is source-available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). That code is the installer, the build-proxy, the host tools, the API, the portal, and the site. Anyone can read it, build it, and change it. Using it for a commercial purpose needs a commercial license from After Dark Systems, LLC.

## Free, under the noncommercial license

The license text is the authority. In short, it covers:

- **Personal use**, including study, research, hobby projects, and testing on your own machines.
- **Noncommercial organizations**, whatever their funding: charities, public schools and schools for the blind, universities, public research bodies, and government institutions.

## Needs a commercial license

- A business running Blunix on machines that serve its customers or its operations.
- Offering Blunix, the portal, or the API as part of a paid product or service.
- A contractor deploying Blunix for a paying client. Ask us if the client is itself a noncommercial organization; we want schools and nonprofits served.

## What a commercial license adds

- The right to use Blunix commercially.
- Support, with a response time. Not built today: the troubleshoot collector and enrolled machines, when they ship.
- Terms your legal team can sign.

## Ask

Email **licensing@blunix.io**. Say who you are and roughly how many machines you run. Companies contributing code can ask for an entity contributor agreement at the same address.

## What this does not change

- Debian's packages inside Blunix images keep their own licenses, many of them the GNU GPL. The commercial license covers only After Dark Systems' own code. It adds no restriction on the GPL-licensed parts. Their source is available as described in each release's `SOURCES.md`.
- Third-party code and fonts bundled with our code keep their licenses. See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
